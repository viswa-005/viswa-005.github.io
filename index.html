Hi! I'm a graduate researcher at UC San Diego, specializing in robotic perception, computer vision, and autonomous systems. My work spans multi-task deep learning, edge AI deployment, and SLAM — building systems that run on real hardware in the real world.

I've had the chance to work across three continents — at CRAS, INESC TEC in Portugal developing drivers for autonomous surface vehicles, and at IIT Madras validating sensors for an underwater glider. Currently I'm a Graduate Researcher at UCSD's Coordinated Robotics Lab, sponsored by GTI Energy.

Outside the lab, I love photography, wildlife, birdwatching, and spending time in nature.

Currently: Researching SLAM on legged robots and uncertainty-aware 3D perception. Looking for full-time roles in robotics, computer vision, and embodied AI starting mid-2026.
